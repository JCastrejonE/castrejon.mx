Physarum — a living transit map

In 2010, Tero and colleagues set oat flakes out in the pattern of the cities around Tokyo, and the slime mold Physarum polycephalum grew a network close to the real rail system. The same year Jeff Jones published a model of the mold built from simple agents that sense and follow a shared chemical trail. This page runs that model on up to a million agents.
